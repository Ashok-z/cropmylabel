1. What this tool is

ShipLabelCrop is a free, browser-based utility that crops A4/Letter shipping label PDFs to 4×6 inch pages for thermal printers. All processing happens on your own device.

2. Acceptable use

3. Output accuracy

The tool does its best to detect label areas and produce correctly sized 4×6 output, but you are responsible for checking each PDF before printing — in particular, print at 100% scale with the paper size set to 4×6 in (101.6 × 152.4 mm), and verify barcodes scan.
