Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version

ShipLabelCrop is a client-side tool. The PDFs you open are processed entirely in your own browser — your label files never leave your device, and we never see, collect, or store them.

What we do not collect

  • Your files. Label PDFs you open with the crop tool are read by JavaScript running locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to any server, and no copy is created anywhere but in your browser's memory.
  • Accounts or identity. There is no sign-up, no login, and no profile. We do not ask for your name, email, or address.
  • Tracking of your activity. We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views and basic usage events — for example, that a file was imported or a label was downloaded, and how many pages it had. These events carry counts only: never file names, file contents, or anything from your PDFs. Umami sets no cookies, does not fingerprint you, and does not track you across sites. We do not use advertising cookies.

What may be collected by third parties

Like most websites, this site relies on third-party infrastructure that may observe standard web-request metadata:

  • Hosting (Vercel). Pages are served over HTTPS by our hosting provider, which may keep short-term server logs (such as IP addresses and request timestamps) for security and reliability purposes.
  • Libraries (cdnjs / Cloudflare). The PDF-processing libraries are loaded from a public CDN; the CDN sees ordinary page-load requests as it would for any site.
  • Analytics (Umami Cloud). The Umami script is loaded from cloud.umami.is and sends the anonymous usage counts described above. Umami stores them in aggregated form; no raw browsing profile is built.

We do not combine this metadata with any personal information, because we do not hold any personal information about you.
